## David Voinov

Third-year student of Data Science and Business Analytics at HSE University (Moscow), minor in Business.
I work with statistics, simulation and Python and look for problems where quantitative analysis meets financial markets and business decisions.

### Research

- **Standing Liquidity Backstops in FX Markets** (lead author, manuscript in preparation)
  Agent-based model of the EUR/USD spot market with dealers, a limit order book and an automated market maker (AMM) liquidity facility, used to compare liquidity backstop designs under matched capital.
- **Trust and Promise Keeping in Language Model Agents** (co-author, manuscript in preparation)
  Controlled experiments with LLM agents in economic games, run on several language models, to test what drives trust and promise keeping.

### Projects

- **Visual-novel learning platform** for the course "Business and Management in Global Context". Solo project, from idea to implementation.
- **T-Bank Latin America market-entry case**. Led a team of 4, ranked markets with a weighted scoring model and built the entry strategy with TOWS, VRIO, a risk matrix and a phased roadmap.
